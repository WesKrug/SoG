- In [7062 IC](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/4562_AR), a group of loggers from [Sze](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Sze) that included the son of Sze's leader [Chou Mingxia](https://pathfinderwiki.com/w/index.php?title=Chou_Mingxia&action=edit&redlink=1) attempted to harvest ancient [sugi](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Sugi) trees at the monastery's site, but returned to town as hostile [undead](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Undead). The townsfolk fended them off in a deadly battle later known as the **Night of Broken Blades**. The abandoned monastery was subsequently regarded as cursed and avoided,though many of the town's residents remained faithful to Sangpotshi
