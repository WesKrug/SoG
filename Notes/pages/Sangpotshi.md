- The philosophy of Sangpotshi, also known as the River of Life, 
  centers around concepts of fate, karma, and cyclic reincarnation. 
  Adherents believe each soul is judged upon its actions in life, and 
  these actions determine its station when it returns to the living world.
   This cycle repeats until the soul reaches its perfected form through 
  the accrual of experience and wisdom, whereupon it passes on to 
  Pharasma’s court and the afterlife to come.
